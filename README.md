# Yubit ~ A JSL Fingerspelling Trainer

## Yubit

In JSL (Japanese Sign Language), they often use fingerspelling to spell out words that do not have a specific sign or to clarify the meaning of a sign. But fingerspelling can be challenging for beginners, especially when it comes to recognizing the handshapes and movements of the letters. To help learners improve their fingerspelling skills, I have developed a web application called Yubit.

Similar apps featuring JSL fingerspelling exist, but most of them only provide static images with no animation or transitions between letters. Yubit, on the other hand, uses 3D models and animations to show how each letter is formed and how they transition from one to another. This will make it easier for learners to understand the handshapes and movements of the letters.
